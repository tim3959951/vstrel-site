Paibook

Internal use

An AI bookkeeping assistant in LINE for small businesses in Taiwan. Snap or upload a receipt, invoice or e-invoice and it is recorded, categorised and checked for input-VAT deductibility. Business-tax (401) figures and financial statements are compiled automatically, with reminders before each filing deadline.

Paibook is a tool for businesses to keep their own books and file their own returns; it does not provide bookkeeping or tax-filing services on anyone's behalf. It is currently used only within our own company.

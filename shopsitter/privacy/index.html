1. 蒐集者與適用範圍

本政策適用於：ShopSitter 的網站（包含 vstrel.com/shopsitter、介紹頁、填表試用頁 /start、試用頁、接上 LINE 的設定頁及付款頁）、ShopSitter 自己的 LINE 官方帳號（@692vyhnp），店家接上 ShopSitter 的 LINE 官方帳號或 Instagram 帳號上的對話，以及店家選擇連結的 Google 日曆（開放後）。

  • 維護服務安全、防止濫用、處理客服與爭議（一三六 資（通）訊與資料庫管理）。
  • 以去識別化的方式統計用量與費用，改善服務（一五七 調查、統計與研究分析）。

本公司不會把個人資料賣給任何人，也不會用客人的資料投放廣告或做行銷。

5. AI 模型怎麼使用您的資料

  • 為了產生回覆，客人的訊息、相關的對話紀錄和店家資料會傳送給 AI 模型服務商（主要為 Anthropic 的 Claude，備援為 Google 的 Gemini）處理。
  • 本公司使用各服務商的付費 API；依其對付費 API 的條款，傳送的內容預設不會被拿去訓練其模型。
  • AI 只依店家提供的資料回答；價格、時間等由程式計算；不確定的事會轉給店家處理。預約是否成立，是由程式依店家設定的規則檢查時段後決定（店家也可以選擇每筆都自己確認），不是由 AI 自行決定；訂單一律要店家確認才算數。
  • 店家連結 Google 日曆時，忙碌時段只由程式用來算出可以預約的時間，不會傳給 AI 模型服務商；AI 只會知道某個時間能不能約。

您可以向本公司請求：查詢或閱覽、製給複製本、補充或更正、停止蒐集處理或利用、刪除您的個人資料。請寄信到 service@vstrel.com，本公司確認您的身分後，於法定期間內處理（查詢或閱覽 15 日內；其他請求 30 日內）。

8. 不提供資料的影響

您可以自由決定是否提供個人資料。不提供必要的資料（例如預約需要的稱呼與電話），店家可能無法完成您的預約或訂單；店家不提供付款資料，無法開通付費方案。

9. Cookie 與本機儲存

ShopSitter 的網頁不使用 Cookie 追蹤您，也不放廣告追蹤碼。/start 頁面會把您還沒送出的表單草稿存在您自己瀏覽器的本機儲存（localStorage），送出前不會傳給本公司；您可以隨時清除瀏覽器資料刪除它。

10. 資料安全

  • 全站使用 HTTPS 加密連線。
  • LINE Channel secret、Instagram 存取權杖與 Google 授權權杖以 AES-GCM 加密後保存。
  • 連結 Google 日曆只申請兩項最小權限：查看忙碌時段，以及管理 ShopSitter 自己建立的日曆；本公司看不到、也改不了店家其他日曆上的行程。
  • 試用頁、接上 LINE 的設定頁及付款頁，都要店家專屬的連結金鑰才能開啟。
  • LINE、Meta 及 SHOPLINE Payments 傳來的通知都會驗證簽章。
  • 只有本公司經授權的人員，為了客服、除錯或法令要求時才能存取資料。

11. 未成年人

ShopSitter 是提供給店家使用的服務，店家應為成年人或依法設立的公司行號。店家的客人未成年時，請由法定代理人同意後再提供預約等個人資料。

ShopSitter Privacy Policy

v1.1 · Effective 16 October 2026

ShopSitter is an AI shop assistant for LINE provided by VSTREL CO., LTD. (維斯托有限公司, "we"). A business connects its own LINE Official Account (and, once available, its Instagram professional account) to ShopSitter; the AI answers the business's customers and takes bookings and orders, and the owner manages it by chatting in their own LINE. A business can also connect its Google Calendar (once available). This policy explains how we handle personal data under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). The Chinese version above prevails if the two differ.

1. Who we are and our role

  • Businesses and their managers: we are the controller of your personal data.
  • Customers of a business (people who message a business account connected to ShopSitter): the business is the controller; we process your data on its behalf, only as needed to provide ShopSitter and on its instructions (PDPA Art. 4). Please contact the business first; you can also contact us and we will help.

2. Data we collect

  • Businesses: business details entered on our form or in the manager chat (name, services and prices, hours, address, phone, FAQs, staff names); managers' LINE user IDs and manager chat messages; LINE channel ID and channel secret; Instagram account ID, username and access token (once available); if the business connects Google Calendar (once available), the Google account's email address, an authorization token and the busy times on its primary calendar (start and end times only: we cannot see event titles, details, locations or guests); receipt email, tax ID and invoice title; payment records and card brand and last four digits. Full card numbers, expiry dates and security codes go directly to SHOPLINE Payments; we never receive or store them.
  • Customers of a business: the LINE user ID or Instagram-scoped ID (IGSID) sent with each message; message content (voice messages are passed to the AI to understand them and are not stored as recordings); details given to book, order, sign up, ask for a quote or subscribe to updates (name, phone, date and time, party size, service or item, notes, topic). We do not request customers' profiles, friend lists or location.
  • Website visitors: a one-way hash of your IP address, used for one day to limit abuse (we do not store the IP address); conversations typed on a trial page (visible only to people with that trial link).

3. Why we use it

To provide ShopSitter (AI replies, bookings and orders, notifications, chat-based management); to bill and issue invoices; to keep the service secure and handle support and disputes; and to produce de-identified usage statistics. We do not sell personal data and do not use customers' data for advertising or marketing.

4. How long we keep it, where, and who receives it

  • Chat history: deleted automatically 30 days after the last message. Manager chat: 1 day.
  • Bookings, orders, sign-ups, enquiries and update subscriptions: until the business deletes them, you or the business ask us to delete them, or within 30 days after the service ends. Time-slot records: 200 days.
  • Business details and change history: while the service is active; deleted within 30 days after it ends or on request. LINE channel secrets and Instagram tokens: deleted when the account is replaced, disconnected or the service ends.
  • Google Calendar: the authorization token and connected email address are deleted when the business disconnects or the service ends; if access is removed in the Google account, the token is deleted as soon as Google refuses it. Busy times are cached for 2 minutes. Bookings written to the business's “ShopSitter 預約” calendar live in the business's own Google account: we delete an event when its booking is cancelled or declined, and events already written stay in the business's calendar after it disconnects.
  • Payment and invoice records: 5 years, as required by Taiwan's accounting laws. IP hashes: 1 day.
  • Data is processed outside Taiwan by the providers below. Recipients: us, the business (for its customers' data), the providers below, and authorities where the law requires.

5. AI models

To generate replies, customer messages, relevant chat history and the business's details are sent to AI model providers (mainly Anthropic's Claude, with Google's Gemini as a backup). We use their paid APIs; under their terms for paid APIs, content is not used to train their models by default. Whether a booking is confirmed is decided by code checking the business's rules (or by the business itself, if it chooses to confirm each one), not by the AI; orders only count once the business confirms them. Google Calendar busy times are used only by code to work out which times are free and are never sent to AI model providers.

6. Service providers

Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting and database, database in the Asia-Pacific region); Anthropic, PBC and Google LLC (AI models); Google LLC (Google Calendar, when a business connects it: reading busy times, and writing bookings — date and time, service, customer name and phone, notes, booking number — to the business's ShopSitter calendar); LY Corporation (LINE messaging); Meta Platforms, Inc. (Instagram messaging, once available); SHOPLINE Payments (card payments and monthly billing); Amego e-invoice service (光貿科技股份有限公司), our e-invoice provider. E-invoices are issued by VSTREL Co., Ltd. and uploaded through Amego to the Ministry of Finance e-invoice platform, as required by Taiwan law.

Google user data

ShopSitter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Calendar is used only to prevent double bookings and keep bookings in sync. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models; it is not transferred to others except as needed to provide these features or to comply with the law; and our staff do not read it unless the business agrees, or it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

7. Your rights

Under PDPA Art. 3 you may ask to access, get a copy of, correct, stop the processing of, or delete your personal data. Email service@vstrel.com; we verify your identity and respond within the legal time limits. See also our data deletion instructions.

8. Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking cookies or ad trackers. The /start page keeps your unsent form draft in your own browser's localStorage; it is not sent to us until you submit.

9. Security

HTTPS everywhere; LINE channel secrets, Instagram tokens and Google authorization tokens are encrypted with AES-GCM; Google Calendar access is limited to two narrow permissions (seeing busy times, and managing the calendar ShopSitter creates), so we cannot see or change events on the business's other calendars; trial, setup and payment pages require a private link key; webhooks from LINE, Meta and SHOPLINE Payments are signature-checked; only authorised staff can access data, for support, debugging or legal requests.

10. Minors

ShopSitter is a service for businesses, which must be adults or registered companies. If a business's customer is a minor, a parent or guardian should agree before booking details are provided.
